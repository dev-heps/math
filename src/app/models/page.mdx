# Applied Models Template

This page provides the **standard recording format** for applied mathematical models, nonlinear dynamical systems, and biophysical equations.

---

## Template Structure

When documenting mathematical models, follow this standard layout:

### 1. Biological / Physical Context & Assumptions
- **Phenomenon**: Brief description of the biological or physical system.
- **State Variables**: Table of variables $\mathbf{x}(t) = [x_1(t), x_2(t), \dots]^T$ with physical dimensions.
- **Key Assumptions**: Underlying conservation laws, mass-action kinetics, spatial homogeneity.

### 2. Governing Equations

$$\begin{cases}
\dfrac{dx_1}{dt} = f_1(x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n; \boldsymbol{\theta}) \\[6pt]
\dfrac{dx_2}{dt} = f_2(x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n; \boldsymbol{\theta})
\end{cases}$$

### 3. Parameters & Dimensions
| Symbol | Meaning | Units | Typical Range |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $\alpha$ | Growth / Production rate | $\text{time}^{-1}$ | $0.1 - 1.0$ |
| $\beta$ | Interaction / Clearance rate | $\text{conc}^{-1} \cdot \text{time}^{-1}$ | $0.01 - 0.5$ |

### 4. Dynamical Analysis
- **Fixed Points (Equilibria)**: Solutions to $\mathbf{f}(\mathbf{x}^*) = \mathbf{0}$.
- **Jacobian Matrix**: $J_{ij} = \frac{\partial f_i}{\partial x_j}\Big|_{\mathbf{x}^*}$.
- **Linear Stability**: Eigenvalues $\lambda_i$ of $J(\mathbf{x}^*)$ and bifurcation classification.
