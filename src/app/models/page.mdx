# Mathematical Biology & Modeling

Applied mathematical modeling uses differential equations, dynamical systems, and stochastic processes to understand biophysical, epidemiological, and cellular phenomena.

## Research Themes

### 1. Nonlinear Population Dynamics
Classic predator-prey systems and multi-species ecological interactions modeled via coupled nonlinear ODEs:

$$\begin{cases}
\dfrac{dx}{dt} = \alpha x - \beta x y \\[8pt]
\dfrac{dy}{dt} = \delta x y - \gamma y
\end{cases}$$

- **Phase Plane Analysis**: Finding fixed points, evaluating Jacobian matrices $J(x^*, y^*)$, and classifying stability (eigenvalues $\lambda_i$).
- **Conservation Laws**: Deriving first integrals $V(x,y)$ to prove the existence of closed orbits.
