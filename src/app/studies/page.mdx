# Studies & Theoretical Foundations

A systematic record of notes and proofs exploring fundamental mathematical structures across analysis, algebra, and geometry.

## Subject Areas

### 1. Real Analysis & Measure Theory
- **Completeness & Metric Topologies**: Metric spaces, Cauchy sequences, Baire Category Theorem, and compactness.
- **Lebesgue Integration**: Measure spaces $(X, \Sigma, \mu)$, measurable functions, Monotone Convergence Theorem, Dominated Convergence Theorem.
- **$L^p$ Spaces & Duality**: Hölder and Minkowski inequalities, completeness ($L^p$ as Banach spaces), and Riesz Representation.

<div className="theorem-box theorem-box--def">
  <span className="theorem-label">Definition — Lebesgue Integrability</span>
  <p>Let $(X, \Sigma, \mu)$ be a measure space. A measurable function $f: X 	o \mathbb{R}$ is Lebesgue integrable if $\int_X |f| \, d\mu < \infty$. The space of all such functions modulo almost everywhere equivalence forms the Banach space $L^1(X, \mu)$.</p>
</div>

### 2. Linear & Multilinear Algebra
- **Vector Spaces & Dimension**: Basis existence (Zorn's lemma), dual spaces $V^*$, quotient spaces $V/W$.
- **Tensor Algebras & Exterior Calculus**: Multilinear maps, tensor product $V \otimes W$, alternating tensors, and the wedge product $\wedge$.

### 3. Topology & Differential Manifolds
- **Point-Set Topology**: Topological spaces, separation axioms ($T_1 \sim T_4$), Urysohn's Lemma, and Tietze Extension.
- **Smooth Manifolds**: Charts, atlases, tangent bundles $TM$, vector fields, and differential forms $\Omega^k(M)$.

<div className="theorem-box">
  <span className="theorem-label">Theorem — General Stokes' Theorem</span>
  <p>Let $M$ be a compact, oriented $n$-dimensional smooth manifold with boundary $\partial M$, and let $\omega$ be a smooth $(n-1)$-form on $M$. Then:</p>
  $$\int_{\partial M} \omega = \int_M d\omega$$
</div>
