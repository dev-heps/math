# Mathematical Studies & Foundations

This section forms the core theoretical backbone of the mathematical archive. It records structured study notes, rigorous proofs, and foundational theorems from undergraduate analysis to graduate-level mathematics.

---

## 1. Real Analysis & Measure Theory

The study of rigorous limits, topologies on $\mathbb{R}^n$, abstract measure spaces, and functional integration.

### A. Completeness & Metric Topologies
- **Axiom of Completeness**: Supremum property, Dedekind cuts, and the construction of $\mathbb{R}$.
- **Metric Space Topology**: Open/closed sets, limit points, compactness, and the **Heine-Borel Theorem**.
- **Completeness & Baire Category**: Cauchy sequences, complete metric spaces (Banach fixed point theorem), and the Baire Category Theorem ($G_\delta$ density).

### B. Lebesgue Measure & Abstract Integration
- **Outer Measures & Carathéodory Extension**: Constructing Lebesgue measure on $\mathbb{R}^n$ via null-sets and $\sigma$-algebras $\mathcal{M}$.
- **Measurable Functions**: Pointwise limits of measurable functions, Lusin's and Egorov's Theorems.
- **Lebesgue Integration & Convergence Theorems**:
  - **Monotone Convergence Theorem (MCT)**: $\lim_{n\to\infty} \int f_n = \int \lim_{n\to\infty} f_n$ for $f_n \ge 0$ monotone.
  - **Fatou's Lemma**: $\int \liminf f_n \le \liminf \int f_n$.
  - **Dominated Convergence Theorem (DCT)**: Pointwise convergence with an integrable dominating function $g \in L^1$.

<div className="theorem-box theorem-box--def">
  <span className="theorem-label">Definition — Abstract Measure Space</span>
  <p>A measure space is a triple $(X, \Sigma, \mu)$ where $X$ is a set, $\Sigma \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)$ is a $\sigma$-algebra on $X$, and $\mu: \Sigma \to [0, \infty]$ is a countably additive set function satisfying $\mu(\emptyset) = 0$.</p>
</div>

### C. $L^p$ Spaces & Duality
- **Normed Linear Spaces of Functions**: Definition of $\|f\|_p = \left(\int_X |f|^p \, d\mu\right)^{1/p}$ for $1 \le p < \infty$.
- **Fundamental Inequalities**:
  - **Hölder's Inequality**: $\|fg\|_1 \le \|f\|_p \|g\|_q$ where $\frac{1}{p} + \frac{1}{q} = 1$.
  - **Minkowski's Inequality**: $\|f + g\|_p \le \|f\|_p + \|g\|_p$.
- **Completeness**: Riesz-Fischer theorem proving $L^p$ is a complete Banach space.
- **Riesz Representation Theorem**: Duality isomorphism $(L^p)^* \cong L^q$ for $1 < p < \infty$.

## 2. Linear & Multilinear Algebra

The algebraic theory of linear transformations, dual spaces, tensor algebras, and canonical forms.

### A. Vector Spaces & Linear Mappings
- **Bases & Dimension**: Linear independence, spanning sets, existence of Hamel bases via Zorn's Lemma.
- **Quotient Spaces & Exact Sequences**: $V / W$ and the first isomorphism theorem for vector spaces.
- **Dual Spaces & Annihilators**: $V^* = \operatorname{Hom}(V, \mathbb{F})$, transpose maps $T^*$, and the canonical embedding $V \hookrightarrow V^{**}$.

### B. Inner Product Spaces & Spectral Theory
- **Hilbert Spaces**: Orthogonal projections, Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization, and Bessel's inequality.
- **Spectral Theorem**: Diagonalization of normal and self-adjoint operators in finite and infinite dimensions.

### C. Multilinear Algebra & Tensor Products
- **Tensor Product $V \otimes W$**: Universal property of bilinear maps $\phi: V \times W \to V \otimes W$.
- **Exterior Algebra $\bigwedge^k V$**: Alternating multilinear forms, the wedge product $v_1 \wedge \cdots \wedge v_k$, and determinant characterization.

<div className="theorem-box">
  <span className="theorem-label">Theorem — Universal Property of Tensor Products</span>
  <p>Let $V, W$ be vector spaces over $\mathbb{F}$. There exists a unique (up to unique isomorphism) vector space $V \otimes W$ and a bilinear map $\iota: V \times W \to V \otimes W$ such that for any bilinear map $f: V \times W \to U$, there exists a unique linear map $\tilde{f}: V \otimes W \to U$ satisfying $f = \tilde{f} \circ \iota$.</p>
</div>

## 3. Topology & Differential Manifolds

Moving from general point-set topology to differential forms and calculus on curved manifolds.

### A. Point-Set Topology
- **Topological Spaces**: Bases, subbases, product topology, and quotient topology.
- **Separation Axioms**: Hausdorff ($T_2$), Regular ($T_3$), and Normal ($T_4$) spaces; **Urysohn's Lemma** and **Tietze Extension Theorem**.
- **Compactness**: Tychonoff's Theorem for arbitrary products, and Alexandroff One-Point Compactification.

### B. Smooth Manifolds & Tangent Bundles
- **Smooth Manifolds**: Atlases, compatible charts $(U_\alpha, \phi_\alpha)$, smooth functions $C^\infty(M)$.
- **Tangent Space $T_p M$**: Derivations on germs of smooth functions, tangent bundle $TM$, and pushforward $d\phi_p$.

### C. Differential Forms & Stokes' Theorem
- **Differential $k$-forms $\Omega^k(M)$**: Smooth sections of the exterior power $\bigwedge^k (T^* M)$.
- **Exterior Derivative $d$**: The unique $\mathbb{R}$-linear operator $d: \Omega^k(M) \to \Omega^{k+1}(M)$ satisfying $d^2 = 0$ and the graded Leibniz rule.
- **Integration & Stokes' Theorem**: Boundary orientation and generalized integration.

<div className="theorem-box">
  <span className="theorem-label">Theorem — Generalized Stokes' Theorem</span>
  <p>Let $M$ be an oriented, compact $n$-dimensional smooth manifold with boundary $\partial M$, and let $\omega \in \Omega^{n-1}(M)$ be a smooth $(n-1)$-form with compact support. Then:</p>
  $$\int_{\partial M} \omega = \int_M d\omega$$
</div>
