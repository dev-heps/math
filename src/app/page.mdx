## Theoretical Focus

Mathematics provides the language and rigorous foundation for modeling complex living and quantum systems. The explorations here center on:

1. **Rigorous Analysis**: Moving from point-set topology to abstract measure theory, function spaces ($L^p, H^s$), and differential forms on manifolds.
2. **Computational Logic & Verification**: Translating classical pen-and-paper proofs into constructive and dependent type theory using **Lean 4**.
3. **Continuous & Discrete Dynamics**: Investigating stability, bifurcations, and emergent patterns in biological networks.
